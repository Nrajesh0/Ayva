# Trademark Policy and Brand Guidelines

This document outlines the trademark policy and guidelines for the **RuN** project (formerly known as **Ayva** / **Focus by Rj**), including its names, logos, app icons, and visual assets.

---

## 1. Our Principles

The source code and documentation of this project are maintained with a commitment to privacy, user autonomy, and transparent development. However, protecting the identity and integrity of the project requires that users always know whether an application they download is an **official, authentic build** or an independent derivative.

Therefore, while software licenses grant permissions regarding the underlying code, **they do NOT grant trademark rights or licenses to use our brand names, trademarks, logos, or icon artwork.**

---

## 2. Project Trademarks & Brand Assets

The trademarks covered by this policy include, but are not limited to:
- **Wordmarks**:
  - `RuN`™
  - `Ayva`™
  - `Focus by Rj`™
- **Logos & Emblems**:
  - The stylized dynamic runner emblem / brand mark.
  - The official adaptive app launcher icons (`ic_launcher`, `app_icon.webp`).
  - The master project vectors (`icon.svg`, `applogo.svg`).
  - Play Store / Fastlane store graphics and badge artwork.

---

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Explicit Permission Needed)

You may use the RuN name and official logo under nominative fair use in the following circumstances:

1. **Factual Reference & Attribution**:
   - Accurately stating that your content, review, or article is about RuN.
   - Example: *"A review of RuN: The Offline Focus & Privacy App"*.
2. **Compatibility & Interoperability**:
   - Indicating that a tool, script, or configuration is compatible with RuN.
   - Example: *"Export utility designed for RuN focus archives"*.
3. **Linking to Official Resources**:
   - Using the official logo or name as a hyperlink directing users to the official GitHub repository (`https://github.com/Nrajesh0/Ayva`) or the official landing page (`https://Nrajesh0.github.io/Ayva/`).
4. **Distribution of Unmodified Official Binaries**:
   - Redistributing official, unmodified release APKs digitally signed by the original maintainer, provided no fees are charged for the application itself and no claims of endorsement or official affiliation are made.

---

## 4. Restricted Uses (Requires Written Authorization)

Without prior written permission from the project maintainer, you may **NOT**:

1. **Impersonate or Imply Endorsement**:
   - Use our name or logo in any manner that falsely implies official sponsorship, partnership, endorsement, or certification by the project creator.
2. **App Store Publishing with Official Branding**:
   - Upload builds to Google Play Store, F-Droid, Amazon Appstore, or third-party marketplaces using the name "RuN", "Ayva", or the official app icon.
3. **Commercial Merchandise & Domain Names**:
   - Register domain names, social media handles, or trademark registrations containing "RuN Focus", "Ayva App", or confusingly similar variations.
   - Sell merchandise or physical goods displaying the official emblem.

---

## 5. Guidelines for Forks and Derivative Works

We respect the freedom to fork and modify the codebase for personal use or alternative visions. To protect community members from security risks and brand confusion, **all forks and derivative distributions must follow these rebranding requirements**:

1. **Change the Name**: Derivative applications must adopt an entirely distinct name that does not include "RuN", "Ayva", or "Focus by Rj" as the primary title.
   - *Acceptable*: *"ZenTracker (based on the RuN engine)"*
   - *Unacceptable*: *"RuN Pro"*, *"RuN Community Edition"*, *"Ayva 2"*
2. **Replace the App Icon and Logos**: You must remove all official icons (`app_icon.webp`, `ic_app_logo.webp`, `icon.svg`, and related assets) and replace them with your own distinct icon before publishing or distributing your build.
3. **Change the Application ID**: Android package names such as `com.focusbyrj.app` must be changed to your own unique namespace (e.g., `com.example.yourapp`).

---

## 6. Logo Presentation & Integrity

When displaying the official emblem in articles, documentation, or links:
- Maintain original aspect ratios; do not stretch, skew, or distort the mark.
- Do not alter the proportions between the runner sweep and the accent dot.
- Do not combine the logo with third-party logos or trademarks into a composite mark.
- Ensure sufficient contrast against background surfaces (the emblem is optimized in pure white against dark/black backgrounds, or black on clean light backgrounds).
