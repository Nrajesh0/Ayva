Focus by Rj is a strictly offline, privacy-first Android application designed to help you stay focused and manage screen time without compromising your personal data. 

Features:
- Block distracting apps using powerful App Restrictions.
- Schedule Deep Work sessions with timers.
- Schedule automatic locking routines (e.g., Bedtime or Work hours).
- Detailed battery health and usage statistics.
- Secure fallback mechanisms, biometric lock, and device administrator protections to prevent circumvention.

This application is fully open source (GPLv3), contains zero analytics or tracking software, and operates 100% offline. All data is stored locally on your device.
