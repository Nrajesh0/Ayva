Architecture & Philosophy

How RuN is actually built.

Most focus apps require accounts, cloud sync, and expensive subscriptions, while leaking your app usage to third-party ad networks. RuN is an entirely offline personal focus operating system.

01 / OVERLAY ENGINE

Direct Window Overlay (BAL-Proof)

Traditional app lockers rely on startActivity() to pop up a blocking screen. On Android 14, 15, and 16, Google's strict Background Activity Launch (BAL) security rules completely break this approach, resulting in blocked intents and security exceptions.

RuN solves this by injecting a direct TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY view straight into the Android WindowManager in under 15ms. It runs completely outside activity stacks, providing smooth, unbypassable interception across all modern Android versions.

Standard App Lockers: ❌ Crashed by Android 14+ BAL
RuN Direct Overlay: ✅ Native WindowManager Bypass
02 / PRIVACY DESIGN

Strict Zero-Network Guarantee

RuN does not request internet permission for its core functions. Your habits, restricted app packages, private notes, and screen time statistics are stored exclusively in an encrypted local SQLite database on your device.

There are zero remote telemetry SDKs, zero crash reporters sending stack traces, and zero analytics pings. We cannot see your data even if we wanted to.

Zero Telemetry No Cloud Accounts Offline-First F-Droid Ready
03 / MIND VAULT

Encrypted Note Vault & Thought Capture

When you stop mindless scrolling, you need an intentional place to put your thoughts. RuN integrates a full-featured markdown and audio note-taking system styled after Google Keep and Notesnook.

Private notes are secured with AES-256-GCM encryption and Argon2id key derivation. Features voice memos, checklists, tables, and encrypted local backup snapshots.

AES-256-GCM Argon2id Voice Memos Encrypted V4 Snapshots
04 / FOCUS ECONOMY

Habit Loops & Mental Sharpening

Dopamine cravings can't just be suppressed—they must be redirected. RuN features daily streak tracking, daily quest milestones, and gamified brain drills (mental arithmetic and aptitude puzzles).

Instead of opening TikTok out of boredom, solve a quick 60-second math drill or complete a habit loop to earn XP, level up your profile, and unlock new profile emblems.
