Built for Sovereign Focus & Uncompromising Privacy

Ayva unites high-discipline time-management, natural conversational task capture, and hardware-level cryptography into one single, self-contained Android engine.

💬

Ayva Bubble Chat & Effortless Task Creation

Never context-switch again. Ayva lives in a lightweight floating overlay accessible across any screen. Simply speak or type natural phrasing like "Buy organic coffee tomorrow at 9am priority"—Ayva's offline NLU automatically extracts the date, time, priority flags, and recurring patterns without sending a single byte to any cloud.

$ ayva --intent-detect "Review contracts Monday 10am persistent"
✓ Parsed: Title="Review contracts", Due="Next Monday, 10:00 AM", Alarm="Persistent Ringing"
📋

Intelligent Tasks & Subtasks

Organized across Today, Upcoming, All, and Occasions. Features full hierarchical subtask checklists, 4-second safety undo banners, and home-screen Android widgets with zero battery overhead.

🛑

Hardware BAL-Immune Blocker

Standard blockers break on Android 14+ due to Background Activity Launch (BAL) restrictions. Ayva uses direct WindowManager.addView(TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY) to project an impenetrable 80px curved mindful pause barrier with ticking countdowns and arithmetic unlock drills.

📝

Encrypted Notes & Voice Vault

Google Keep-inspired aesthetic meets zero-knowledge cryptography. Features colored note cards, embedded checklists, and an on-device audio memo recorder with animated waveform playback.

⚡

Habits & Smart Notifications

Build positive rhythms and break compulsive habits with streak multipliers and equipped Streak Freezes. Precise notifications via Android's native AlarmManager with zero telemetry.

🛡️

Point-in-Time Snapshots, Cryptographic Backups & Sovereign Security

Your data never leaves your silicon. Ayva takes automated hourly and daily snapshots for instant 1-tap state rollbacks. Export password-protected offline backups encrypted with Argon2id KDF and AES-256-GCM. Recover your private vault anytime with an industry-standard 12-word BIP-39 mnemonic seed phrase.
