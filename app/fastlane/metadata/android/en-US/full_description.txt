Focus by Rj is an offline, privacy-respecting App Locker and Focus Tracker designed to help you stay productive and maintain screen time boundaries. 

Key Features:
- Fully Offline: No cloud syncing or analytics. All data stays locally on your device.
- App Locking: Restrict access to distracting applications securely.
- Focus Schedules: Set up automated routines for work, study, or sleep where specific apps are blocked automatically.
- Screen Time Tracking: Monitor your usage statistics locally using Android's built-in UsageStatsManager. 
- Privacy First: Operates without requiring invasive Accessibility Services, maintaining your privacy while still keeping you focused.

Stay focused without sacrificing your privacy!
